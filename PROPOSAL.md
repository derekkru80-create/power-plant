# Powermax Gasification Power Plant Proposal
## High Level, Northern Alberta

**Prepared by:** Derek Kruger

**Project Location:** High Level, Northern Alberta  
**Capacity:** 10 MW Pilot Plant

---

## Executive Summary

This proposal outlines a 10 MW Powermax gasification power plant pilot project in High Level, Northern Alberta. This initiative addresses critical regional and provincial challenges including emissions reduction, wildfire management, renewable energy baseload capacity, and sustainable biomass utilization.

The project will convert post-fire timber salvage and logging residues into clean, renewable baseload power while producing valuable biochar byproducts. This represents a sustainable solution to Alberta's energy security and environmental commitments for 2050.

---

## Project Overview

### Vision
Establish a pioneering biomass gasification facility that transforms forest waste into reliable baseload power while generating economic value through biochar production and carbon credit monetization.

### Location Rationale: High Level, Northern Alberta
- **Proximity to Resource:** Direct access to 880,000 hectares of post-fire salvageable timber across Alberta (25 million cubic meters, conservative estimate)
- **Fire Smart Programs:** Supports provincial fire mitigation efforts
- **Infrastructure Access:** Existing road networks and power grid connections
- **Economic Development:** Stimulates local economy and employment opportunities

### Technology: Powermax Gasification System
- **Proven Technology:** Gasification processes have been in development since WWII with significant modern improvements
- **Cost Efficiency:** Substantially more affordable than steam turbines or hydroelectric alternatives
- **Baseload Capability:** Provides consistent, dispatchable power generation
- **Environmental Benefits:** Minimal emissions with carbon sequestration potential

## Resource Assessment

### Available Biomass
- **Post-Fire Timber (Alberta 2023):** 880,000 hectares containing 25 million cubic meters
- **Salvage Window:** 1-2 years before timber loses viability
- **Carbon Impact if Unused:** 1 ton of CO₂ released per cubic meter (through burning or decomposition)
- **Annual Logging Residue:** Top piles burned seasonally (November 1st - Alberta and B.C.)

### Feedstock Sustainability
- Abundant, renewable supply from forest management operations
- Reduces wildfire risk through fuel reduction
- Prevents carbon emissions from forest waste decomposition
